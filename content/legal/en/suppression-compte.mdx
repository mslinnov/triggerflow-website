## Who this page is for

This page explains how to delete a **TriggerFlow** user account, used in the TriggerFlow mobile app (Android and iOS) and on `app.trigger-flow.com`. The app and the service are published by Utelys SAS.

Your account was created by the establishment employing you. You can request its deletion yourself, without going through the app and without reinstalling it.

## Request deletion

**Option 1: by email**

1. Write to **privacy@trigger-flow.com** from your account's email address, with the subject "Delete my account".
2. Give the name of your establishment.
3. We reply to confirm receipt of the request. If the message does not come from the account's address, we may ask you to confirm your identity.

**Option 2: through your establishment**

Your establishment's TriggerFlow administrator can delete your account from the software. Access is then cut off immediately, and the account data is erased within the same 30-day period.

From the mobile app, the "Delete my account" link in Settings opens this page.

## Timeframe

The account is deleted within **30 days** of receiving the request. A confirmation is sent to you by email once the deletion is done. From the deletion onwards, you can no longer log in and your phones are logged out.

## Data deleted

This data is erased, or anonymised when it must stay attached to a history of the establishment:

- Your account: first name, last name, email address, password, two-factor authentication settings, preferences
- Your registered phones and their notification tokens
- Your personal notification and call answering settings

## Data kept

Some data is not deleted with the account, for the following reasons:

| Data | Why | Period |
|---|---|---|
| Work content created for the establishment (messages sent to its guests, notes, tasks, records, attached photos and files) | It belongs to the establishment, controller of this data; it stays with the establishment, detached from your account | According to the establishment's choices |
| Connection and security logs | Service security and fraud prevention | 1 year |
| Billing data (if you are the establishment's billing contact) | Accounting and tax obligation | 10 years |

To have work content deleted, contact your establishment.
