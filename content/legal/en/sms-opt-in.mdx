## Program description

TriggerFlow is a SaaS platform operated by **Utelys SAS** that allows hotels and accommodation providers to send **transactional, service-related SMS** to their own guests. Messages relate exclusively to a guest's **reservation and stay** — for example: booking confirmations, check-in and arrival instructions, room or door access codes, tourist-tax payment, and departure information.

TriggerFlow does **not** send marketing or promotional SMS through this program, and phone numbers are **never sold or shared** with third parties for marketing purposes.

## How guests opt in (consent)

End users are **guests of hotels** that use the TriggerFlow platform. A guest provides their **mobile phone number when making a reservation** with the hotel — through the hotel's online booking engine, an online travel agency (OTA), by phone, or at the front desk.

By providing their mobile number during the booking, the guest **consents to receive transactional service messages** strictly related to their own reservation and stay. Consent is collected by the hotel **at the time of reservation**. Because these are transactional messages tied to a reservation the guest has actively made, the guest expects to receive service communications about that stay.

Receiving these SMS is **not a condition of any purchase**.

## Proof of consent collection

Consent is collected by the hotel, not by Utelys. Because the phone number is
gathered inside each hotel's own booking flow, the opt-in step is not reachable
from a single public link: a guest only sees it after choosing dates and a room,
within an active booking session. The screenshot below therefore documents the
complete consent experience.

Example — the direct booking engine of our customer Château d'Acotz
(`chateauacotz.thais-hotel.com`, operated by the Thais property management
system):

![Contact step of the Château d'Acotz booking engine: the "Téléphone" (phone number) field is mandatory, as is acceptance of the hotel's Terms and Conditions.](/images/legal/sms-opt-in-booking-engine.jpg)

On this final step of the reservation, before any payment:

- **Phone number is a required field** (marked with an asterisk) — the guest
  cannot complete the reservation without providing it.
- **Acceptance of the hotel's Terms and Conditions is required** (checkbox
  marked with an asterisk), and those terms cover the service communications
  relating to the stay.
- A separate, **optional** checkbox covers commercial contact. It is not used by
  this program: the messages described here are transactional only, and are sent
  whether or not that optional box is ticked.

The same information is collected through the other booking paths — online
travel agencies, telephone, and the front desk — and transmitted to the hotel's
property management system, which is where TriggerFlow reads it from.

## Types of messages

- Booking and reservation confirmations
- Check-in and arrival instructions
- Room or door access codes
- Tourist-tax payment requests
- Departure and check-out information
- Other notifications directly related to the guest's stay

## Message frequency

Message frequency **varies depending on the reservation and the stay** — typically a small number of messages per stay (for example, around the time of booking, arrival, and departure).

## Fees

**Message and data rates may apply**, depending on the guest's mobile carrier and plan.

## Opt-out and help

- **Opt-out:** a guest can unsubscribe at any time by replying **STOP** to any message. No further messages will be sent.
- **Help:** a guest can reply **HELP** for assistance, or contact us at **privacy@trigger-flow.com**.

## Privacy

Phone numbers are processed solely to deliver the transactional messages described above and are never sold or shared with third parties for marketing. For full details on how personal data is handled, see our [Privacy Policy](/en/politique-confidentialite).
