## Introduction

This policy covers the **TriggerFlow** mobile app for Android and iOS (the "App"). The App is reserved for the staff of TriggerFlow customers (hotels, accommodation providers, restaurants). It does not allow account creation: each account is created by the establishment employing the person, from the TriggerFlow software.

It supplements the general TriggerFlow [Privacy Policy](/en/politique-confidentialite), which applies to anything not specified here.

Two roles must be distinguished:

- For your account data and the operation of the App, Utelys acts as **data controller**.
- For the data of the establishment's guests displayed in the App (bookings, messages, calls), Utelys acts as **processor** on behalf of the establishment, under the [Data Processing Agreement](/en/dpa) signed with it.

## 2. Data processed by the App

### 2.1 Your account

- First name, last name, business email address, role and establishment(s)
- Password (stored with irreversible hashing) and two-factor authentication settings
- Preferences: language, notifications

### 2.2 Your phone

- Phone model, operating system (Android or iOS), App version
- Installation identifier randomly generated by the App
- Notification tokens issued by Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and Apple (Apple Push Notification service), needed to ring the phone and receive notifications
- Date of last connection

The App does not collect your location, does not read the phone's advertising identifier and contains no advertising or audience measurement tool.

### 2.3 Calls

When the establishment enables call answering in the App:

- Caller number, date, call duration, person who answered
- Voice is transmitted in real time during the call; the microphone is only used during a call you have accepted
- A call is only recorded if the establishment has enabled recording (off by default); voicemails left by callers may be transcribed and translated

### 2.4 Your work content

- Messages exchanged with the establishment's guests, tasks, housekeeping, guest and prospect records you view or enter
- Photos you take with the App (for example a business card) and files you choose to attach to a record
- Phone contacts: read only when you choose to import a contact; only the selected contact's details are sent, never the whole address book

### 2.5 Offline use

To remain usable without a network, the App keeps recently viewed records and entries waiting to be sent on the phone. Entries are sent when the network is back. Logging out erases the data kept on the phone for offline viewing.

### 2.6 Technical logs

Our servers keep connection and security logs (IP address, date, operation performed).

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Identify you and secure access to your account | Performance of the contract with the establishment, legitimate interest in security |
| Ring the phone and route calls from the establishment's guests | Performance of the contract |
| Send the notifications you have enabled | Performance of the contract, consent given on the phone |
| Display and save your work content | Performance of the contract (on behalf of the establishment) |
| Prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents | Legitimate interest |
| Comply with legal obligations | Legal obligation |

Data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared to track your activity across other apps or websites.

## 4. Phone permissions

Each permission is requested when the related feature is used. You can refuse or revoke it at any time in the phone settings; only the related feature then stops working.

| Permission | Use |
|---|---|
| Microphone | Speak during a call you have accepted |
| Notifications | Receive messages, tasks and incoming calls |
| Calls (CallKit on iOS, call management and full screen display on Android) | Show an incoming call as a phone call, including on a locked phone |
| Camera | Take a photo to attach to a record (a business card for example) |
| Photos and files | Attach an image or a file you choose |
| Contacts | Import a contact you choose |

## 5. Recipients and processors

Data is accessible to authorised people of your establishment, according to the rights it has given you, and to Utelys teams for support and maintenance.

| Provider | Role in the App | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Scaleway | Server and data hosting | France (European Union) |
| Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) | Notification delivery on Android | European Union / United States |
| Apple (Apple Push Notification service) | Notification and call delivery on iOS | United States / international |
| Twilio | Call voice routing, recordings and voicemails | European Union (Ireland) / United States |

Other TriggerFlow processors (email and SMS sending, artificial intelligence, etc.) are involved depending on the features used by the establishment; they are listed in the general [Privacy Policy](/en/politique-confidentialite). Transfers outside the European Union are governed by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or by an adequacy decision.

## 6. Retention periods

| Data | Period |
|---|---|
| Account data | As long as the account is active, then deletion according to the [account deletion procedure](/en/suppression-compte) |
| Phone and notification tokens | Until logout, account deletion or removal of the phone by the establishment |
| App session | 30 rolling days without use, then a new login is required |
| Call recordings and voicemails | 30 days (the transcription text stays attached to the establishment's record) |
| Connection and security logs | 1 year |
| Work content (messages, records, photos, files) | According to the choices of the establishment, controller of this data |

## 7. Security

- Encrypted exchanges between the App and our servers (TLS 1.2 minimum)
- Login token stored in the phone's secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore)
- Two-factor authentication available, and mandatory when the establishment requires it
- The establishment can remove a phone's access at any time

## 8. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability provided by the GDPR. To exercise them: privacy@trigger-flow.com, or by post to Utelys SAS, 13 rue Saint-Ursule, 31000 Toulouse, France. Answer within one (1) month.

To delete your account, follow the [account deletion procedure](/en/suppression-compte), also available from the App Settings.

For the data of the establishment's guests, contact the establishment, which is the controller of this data; Utelys helps it answer.

In case of disagreement, you may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

## 9. Audience of the App

The App is intended exclusively for adult professionals. It is not directed at children.
